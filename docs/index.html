Todo Widget GitHub

Your to-dos, always in the menu bar.

A free Mac app for daily, weekly and monthly lists. Click the icon, add a task, and get back to what you were doing.

Install in Terminal

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/andrewcjh27/Todo-Widget/main/scripts/install.sh | bash
Download the zip

Requires macOS 13 or later. The app isn't notarized by Apple, so the zip may show a security warning. The Terminal command is the simpler way to install.

  • Three lists

    Keep daily, weekly and monthly tasks apart, and switch between them in one click.

  • Weekly planner

    Give each day of the week its own list.

  • Due dates

    Add a date to weekly and monthly tasks and see the days left at a glance.

  • Launch at login

    Tick one box and the app starts with your Mac.

  • Stays on your Mac

    Tasks are saved on your computer. No account needed.

  • Free and open source

    Read the code, change it, or build it yourself under the MIT license.
